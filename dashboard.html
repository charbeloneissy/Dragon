🧮 Core execution mathematics

GrossEdge(Q) = (VWAP_sell(Q) − VWAP_buy(Q)) / VWAP_buy(Q) × 10,000
NetEdge = GrossEdge − Fees − Slippage − LatencyPenalty − RebalanceCost − SafetyBuffer
ExpectedPnL = Notional × NetEdge / 10,000
EV = P(win) × Profit − (1 − P(win)) × Loss
Nmax = min(Ncapital, Nliquidity, Nrisk, Nexecution, Nexchange)
RealizedPnL = SellProceeds − BuyCost − Fees − Slippage − RebalanceCost
